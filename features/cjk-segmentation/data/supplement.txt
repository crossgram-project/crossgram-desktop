# Crossgram word supplement for the CJK dictionary.
#
# The base dictionary is a Chinese word list from a news corpus, so everyday
# messaging, application and internet words are missing from it, and words it
# does know are often ranked below the single characters they are made of.
# Every line here is one word, optionally followed by a frequency; a word
# without a frequency gets 3000, which is the weight of a very common word in
# the base list. Frequencies are only compared with each other.
#
# Words are listed in the same spelling both in simplified and in traditional
# Chinese where the two differ, because the base list only knows the
# simplified characters and their runs would otherwise fall apart.
